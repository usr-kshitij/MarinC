Operators & Expressions

Operators are the symbols that let your C program calculate, compare and change values.

LESSON 04
Lesson progress 14%
CHAPTER 01 • OPERATORS

Make your values do something.

Variables hold information. Operators tell C what to do with that information. For example: 10 + 5 tells C to add two numbers.

MARIN
An operator is basically an instruction symbol. + means add. - means subtract. * means multiply. Easy, right? ⚡

04 / IMPORTANT

Operator precedence

C doesn't simply calculate everything from left to right. Some operators are evaluated first.

2 + 3 * 4 = ?
STEP 01 3 * 4

Multiplication happens first.

STEP 02 2 + 12

Now perform the addition.

RESULT 14

So the answer is 14, not 20.
